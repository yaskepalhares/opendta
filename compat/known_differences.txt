0513_tobit            Stata 14's tobit takes the standard errors from a numerical Hessian (~1e-5 relative difference) and stops before full convergence (ll in the 10th digit); coefficients, header and e() match
0801_testes           swilk with tied values (Stata uses coefficients with average ranks, an undocumented rule; without ties it matches) and the kwallis chi2 in the 7th digit (undocumented internal formula; the adjusted chi2, df and displayed p match)
