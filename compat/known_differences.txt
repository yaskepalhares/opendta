# case matches again (for example, after producing the reference again in Stata).
# format: case-name  reason
# or:     case-name  ignore(/REGEX/) reason  -- only the lines matching REGEX may differ
#         case-name  tolerance(REL) reason   -- numbers may differ by REL (relative, at least 1)
# (both limits can be combined; the rest of the log must still be equal)

0406_mata_optimize    with a d0 evaluator, the gradient at the optimum and the last digits of V are noise from the numerical derivatives (~1e-12 and ~1e-8); Stata's step search is not documented in detail. Iteration log, parameters, value and the d1 case match
0507_poisson          with exposure(t), the upper CI limit of _cons differs in the 7th digit (.102692 vs .1026919): Stata reports a point not yet fully converged; coefficients, log and other lines match
0512_nbreg            Stata's starting values are undocumented: iteration 0 of the Poisson comes out -245.3105 (Stata -245.31049) and iterations 0 and 1 of the full model differ; the final point differs in the 8th digit of alpha (robust /lnalpha CI in the 7th). Coefficients, standard errors, tests and e() match
0513_tobit            Stata 14's tobit takes the standard errors from a numerical Hessian (~1e-5 relative difference) and stops before full convergence (ll in the 10th digit); coefficients, header and e() match
0801_testes           swilk with tied values (Stata uses coefficients with average ranks, an undocumented rule; without ties it matches) and the kwallis chi2 in the 7th digit (undocumented internal formula; the adjusted chi2, df and displayed p match)
0802_anova_estat      the estat hettest chi2 is close to zero (9e-5) and Stata computes it with numerical cancellation: it differs from the 11th digit on

0602_svy_ampliado     in svy: nbreg alpha goes to the boundary (ln alpha ≈ -37) and the displayed /lnalpha depends on the optimizer's path (coefficients and standard errors match, as in the Poisson); two numbers differ in the 7th digit (/cut2 of svy: ologit and _cons of svy: glm), noise from the convergence criterion
0804_comandos         the qreg iteration log is not reproduced (Stata's internal algorithm is undocumented; coefficients, standard errors and e() match); xtpoisson, re and xtlogit, re take their standard errors from numerical derivatives and differ in the 7th digit (iteration 1 of xtpoisson, in the 8th); lowess differs by 1 float ulp at two extremes; the hausman chi2 differs in the 11th digit
1201_mi               the imputed values come from OpenDTA's random-number generator (Stata's order of draws in mi impute is undocumented): imputed values and combined estimates differ; layout, counts and the combination rules can be compared
0902_stcox_streg      ignore(/^Iteration \d+: +log (pseudo)?likelihood = +-1[4-9]\d\.|^ +e\(aux_p\) = /) Stata's iteration path for streg with the Weibull, lognormal and loglogistic distributions is undocumented (step sizes of the constant-only Weibull fit and starting values of the location-scale models): the iteration lines differ and e(aux_p) differs in the 10th digit; the estimates, standard errors, tests and the rest of the log match
